# Contributing to PICADS

PICADS is experimental EDA software. Read
[known issues and verification gaps](docs/KNOWN_ISSUES_AND_LIMITATIONS.md).

## Mandatory contributor agreement for new official contributions

**New contributions to the official PICADS repository are accepted only with
an executed [Contributor License Agreement (CLA)](docs/CONTRIBUTOR_AGREEMENT.md).**
The contributor retains copyright, and grants the actual PICADS rightsholder
broad nonexclusive rights to use, distribute and separately license accepted
contributions, including distinct author-controlled editions not previously made publicly
available free. This does not withdraw rights in previously free editions.
The agreement must be signed by the actual rights owner or authorized entity
and countersigned by the PICADS receiving rightsholder. A pull request alone,
or simply mentioning MotionflowOffical in a file, does not give licensing
or sublicensing rights. The agreement applies only when actually executed;
it is NOT automatically imposed on contributions to outside forks.

An employer, joint owner or another IP holder must grant permission where
necessary. Disclose third-party code, AI-generated inputs and known license
or other intellectual-property restrictions. No contribution is accepted
without sufficient rights.

## Development and redistribution standards

- Preserve the PICADS name, original principal creator credit, the official
  source URL https://github.com/MotionflowOffical/PICADS, LICENSE, NOTICE and current WIP warnings.
- Standalone PICADS resale, paywalls and direct paid PICADS hosting require
  actual rightsholder permission. Commercial embedding in an independently
  valuable paid product or platform is permitted ONLY with the entire exact
  modified PICADS component source publicly obtainable for free under
  LICENSE Section 3. A cosmetic wrapper does not qualify.
- Commercial use of PICADS or privately modified PICADS requires original
  creator attribution in product/client documentation, or an internal project
  record if there is no external deliverable; see LICENSE Section 7.
- Internal private modifications need not be disclosed merely because their
  separately generated outputs are sold. Source sharing IS required for any
  distributed PICADS component or third-party-deployed PICADS service.
- Include focused tests and exact reproduction steps with each contribution.
- Update `docs/KNOWN_ISSUES_AND_LIMITATIONS.md` for changed capabilities,
  failures or unverified functionality; never claim manufacturing signoff
  from a mocked test or successful GDS generation alone.
- Disclose modified components, credit authors accurately, and avoid
  destructive operations or unapproved dependency installation.

The CLA grant is **nonexclusive** and does not assign contributor ownership.
No unsigned proposal establishes an agreement; the contributor and recipient
must identify their actual ownership and authority.
